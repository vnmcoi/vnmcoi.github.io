# Marie Curie Olympiad in Informatics (MCOI)

## Abstract
The Marie Curie Olympiad in Informatics (MCOI) is a comprehensive public archive dedicated to the dissemination of knowledge in applied mathematics and theoretical computer science. Operating under the broader VNMCOI namespace, this repository serves as a centralized, open-access pedagogical resource designed to facilitate the study of advanced algorithms, data structures, and competitive programming methodologies.

## Historical Background
Established in early 2025, the MCOI initiative originated as a private, closed-access training repository specifically structured for the competitive programming selection team at Marie Curie High School in Ho Chi Minh City. Its initial objective was to provide rigorous internal training materials and algorithmic problem-solving frameworks. Recognizing the broader educational value of these curated resources, the project was subsequently restructured and transitioned into a fully public archive to benefit the wider academic community.

## Pedagogical Objectives
The primary directive of this repository is to aggregate, synthesize, and organize high-quality educational materials. By maintaining a structured curriculum and a comprehensive reference base, the archive aims to support self-directed learning, foster analytical problem-solving skills, and encourage advanced research in computational mathematics.

## Content Aggregation Policy
The MCOI archive adheres strictly to an open-source aggregation framework. All materials, algorithmic editorials, documentations, and problem sets included within this repository are exclusively derived from publicly available, open-source educational platforms. 

The integration of closed-source, proprietary, or commercially restricted content is strictly prohibited. This rigorous policy ensures that the archive remains permanently accessible, legally compliant, and aligned with the fundamental ethos of open academic knowledge sharing.

## Target Audience
This repository is intended for students, educators, and independent researchers who possess a profound interest in competitive programming, algorithmic optimization, and applied mathematics. It is designed to serve as both a foundational learning tool and an advanced reference manual for complex computational concepts.
